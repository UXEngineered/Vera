## Roadmap guidance

- Use Now / Next / Later horizons, not dates.
- Every item has an `action`:
  - `build` — ship it. Only for items backed by high-confidence evidence (medium is acceptable if the wording says "likely").
  - `validate` — run a test to confirm or kill the idea before building.
  - `investigate` — gather missing evidence; the idea is not yet testable.
- Low-confidence items are never `build`.
- Sequence by risk: validate the riskiest assumptions before committing build effort behind them.
- Keep it achievable for the stated team size and timeline. Do not plan more work than the team can do.
- Aim for 2–4 items per horizon. Fewer if the evidence is thin.
