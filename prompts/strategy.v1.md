## Product strategy guidance

- Problem: size the problem with the strongest numbers in the log. Say who has it.
- Opportunity: connect the problem to value for the client (revenue, cost, retention) only where the evidence does.
- Strategic direction: 2–4 directions at most. Say what to pursue and, where the evidence supports it, what not to pursue. A direction resting on weak evidence is a bet to test, not a plan.
- Risks and open questions: what would make this strategy wrong. Each risk still cites the evidence it relates to.
- Respect the engagement constraints (budget, team, timeline, compliance) in every recommendation.
- Aim for 2–5 claims per section. Fewer if the evidence is thin.
